### Anchor Text Strategy
- Branded (`RELURL`, `relurl.com`): 40%
- Partial-match (`free URL shortener`, `QR code generator`): 30%
- Generic (`this guide`, `read more`, `here`): 20%
- Exact-match (`best free URL shortener`): 10%

### Per-Article Backlink Count
Each article should link to 3-5 different RELURL pages (mix of `/en/url-shortener`, `/en/qr-code-generator`, `/en/pricing`, etc. with `ref=<platform>` param).
