# How to Secure Your Short Links: Password Protection and Expiration

Short links are convenient, but they can also be a security risk if not managed properly. Here is how to secure your links with enterprise-grade protection.

## The Problem with Unsecured Links

Anyone with a short link can access the destination. For internal resources, client deliverables, or exclusive content, this is unacceptable. A [password protected link](https://relurl.com/en/password-protected-links?ref=hackernoon.com) adds a layer of security that prevents unauthorized access.

## Implementing Password Protection

Password-protected links require visitors to enter a password before redirecting to the destination. This is ideal for:
- Client deliverables and proposals
- Internal company resources
- Exclusive content and early access
- Private event pages

## Link Expiration for Time-Sensitive Content

Some links should only work for a limited time. [Link expiration](https://relurl.com/en/link-expiration?ref=hackernoon.com) lets you define exactly when a link stops working — hours, days, or months after creation.

### Use Cases

**Marketing campaigns** use link expiration to enforce deadlines. A [marketing URL shortener](https://relurl.com/en/marketing-url-shortener?ref=hackernoon.com) with expiration ensures limited-time offers remain genuinely limited.

**Event registration** links expire after the event date. An [event link shortener](https://relurl.com/en/event-link-shortener?ref=hackernoon.com) prevents post-event access to registration pages.

**Webinar links** expire after the session. A [webinar link shortener](https://relurl.com/en/webinar-link-shortener?ref=hackernoon.com) creates temporary join links that auto-expire.

## Redirect Types and SEO

Choosing between 301 and 302 redirects matters for SEO. Permanent (301) redirects pass link equity. Temporary (302) redirects are better for campaign links where the destination may change. A [URL tracking tool](https://relurl.com/en/url-tracking-tool?ref=hackernoon.com) lets you choose per link.

For teams managing links together, role-based access prevents unauthorized changes. A [URL shortener for business](https://relurl.com/en/url-shortener-for-business?ref=hackernoon.com) provides admin, editor, and viewer roles.

## Link Health Monitoring

Broken links hurt user experience and SEO. A [URL shortener with analytics](https://relurl.com/en/url-shortener-with-analytics?ref=hackernoon.com) monitors link health and alerts you when destinations become unreachable.

## Custom Domains for Trust

Using your own domain for short links builds trust. A [custom domain link](https://relurl.com/en/custom-domain-links?ref=hackernoon.com) tool makes visitors more likely to click because they recognize your brand.

## Best Practices

1. Always use password protection for sensitive content
2. Set expiration dates for time-sensitive campaigns
3. Use 301 redirects for permanent links
4. Monitor link health regularly
5. Use branded domains for higher click-through rates
