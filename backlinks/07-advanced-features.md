## Link Rot Prevention

Short links from free services can break if the provider goes down. Using a professional URL shortener with custom domains ensures your links remain active and under your control.

## Redirect Types

Choose between 301 (permanent) and 302 (temporary) redirects. Permanent redirects pass full SEO value while temporary ones are better for campaigns and A/B testing.

## Team Collaboration

Enterprise-grade URL shorteners support team workspaces, role-based permissions, and shared link libraries. Ideal for marketing teams and agencies managing multiple client accounts.

## Analytics & Reporting

Advanced analytics go beyond click counts. Track geographic locations, device types, browser data, referrer sources, and engagement time. Export reports for client presentations or campaign analysis.
