QR codes were once dismissed as a failed experiment. Today they are everywhere — restaurant tables, hotel lobbies, business cards, and product packaging. Here is why they matter for your business.

## The Shift to Contactless

The pandemic permanently changed how people interact with physical spaces. A [QR code generator](https://relurl.com/en/qr-code-generator?ref=hackernoon.com) became essential for restaurants, retail stores, and service businesses. But the technology has evolved far beyond simple URL encoding.

## Dynamic vs Static QR Codes

Static QR codes encode the destination URL directly. You cannot change it after printing. A [dynamic QR code generator](https://relurl.com/en/dynamic-qr-code-generator?ref=hackernoon.com) stores the destination on a server and redirects on scan. This means you can update the target URL without reprinting.
